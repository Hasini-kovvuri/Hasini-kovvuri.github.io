Overview

The problem

Hyper-realistic deepfakes make it hard to know whether a photo or video of a person is genuine, or where a face is being reused without consent. Detection tools exist in research, but they're rarely joined up into something an ordinary person can use.

DeepShield-AI checks images and video for manipulation, matches faces against what it has already seen, traces where a face appears on the web, and packages the findings into a report someone can act on.

What it does

Key features

Deepfake detection

A PyTorch ResNet18 classifier fine-tuned on FaceForensics++ returns a Real vs. Deepfake probability, alongside XceptionNet and EfficientNet pipelines.

Video analysis

Uploaded MP4 and WEBM videos are analysed frame by frame with OpenCV.

Face similarity search

FaceNet (MTCNN) turns each face into a 512-dimensional embedding; a FAISS index finds visually similar faces in scan history.

Face Trace

A reverse image search through Google Lens (SerpApi) lists the websites where a face is circulating.

Security dashboard

Live threat alerts, scan analytics and direct webcam capture, all in one dashboard.

Investigation reports

Downloadable PDF reports with the verdict, probabilities, detected sources and a processing timeline.
