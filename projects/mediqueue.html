Overview

The problem

Hospital visits often mean long, unclear waits. Patients don't know when they'll be seen, and front desks spend time juggling bookings by hand.

MediQueue lets patients find a specialist, book a slot and follow their place in a live queue, while doctors manage the day from their own dashboard. Medoc, a Gemini-powered assistant, gives a quick read on symptoms before the visit.

Architecture

How it works

The path a request takes through the system.

Step 1Patient sign-inSecure accounts with AWS Cognito
Step 2Medoc assistantGemini checks symptoms, suggests a doctor
Step 3Book a slotAPI Gateway and Lambda handle bookings
Step 4Live queueDoctor-wise queues with token tracking
Step 5DataDynamoDB and Amazon RDS
What it does

Key features

Specialist directory

Doctors with their specialty, rating, availability, current queue and next free slot.

Real-time queues

Doctor-wise queues with live token tracking, waiting counts and average wait time.

Doctor dashboard

Today's appointments, the current patient and a one-click Call next patient.

Medoc AI assistant

A Gemini chatbot, served by Flask, that explains symptoms simply, rates severity and suggests the right doctor.

Secure authentication

Sign-up and login through AWS Cognito.

DevOps pipeline

Docker containers, Kubernetes orchestration, Jenkins CI/CD and infrastructure provisioned with Terraform.
