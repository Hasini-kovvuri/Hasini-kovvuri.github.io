Overview

The problem

Students starting a new course often don't know how to structure their study, how to check what they've understood, or who to ask when they're stuck.

Cohort Collab gives every learner a structured plan, a way to test themselves and a mentor to ask, all generated by a large language model and shared in study rooms with peers.

Architecture

How it works

The path a request takes through the system.

Step 1Set up an agentChoose a course and level, name your study agent
Step 2Flask APIOne endpoint routes plan, quiz and chat
Step 3Gemini on Vertex AIGemini 2.0 Flash-Lite generates the content
Step 4Structured outputMarkdown plans, JSON quizzes, chat replies
Step 5FirebaseSign-in, stored data and cloud functions
What it does

Key features

Personalized study plans

A 4-week plan for any course and level, laid out with weekly goals and daily tasks.

Auto-generated quizzes

Multiple-choice questions built from the plan's content, returned as structured JSON so the app can render and score them.

AI study mentor

A customizable, named study agent that answers questions and gives structured, encouraging feedback.

Study rooms

Collaborative spaces where learners study together alongside the AI features.

Accounts and data

Firebase Authentication for sign-in and Cloud Firestore for user data.

Progress tracking

Pages for tracking progress through the plan alongside quizzes and chat.
