Hi, I'm Hasini.

I'm a final-year Electronics and Communication Engineering student at Aditya Engineering College, Surampalem, graduating in 2027 with a CGPA of 9.51.

I enjoy taking an idea all the way to a working product: models such as ResNet18 and FaceNet, features on large language models like Google Gemini, APIs in FastAPI and Flask, and deployment on AWS with Docker, Kubernetes, Terraform and Jenkins.
